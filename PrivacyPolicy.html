The App functions 100% offline and locally on your mobile device:

The App does not require sensitive device permissions (such as Camera, Contacts, Microphone, or GPS Location) to operate.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party web services, advertising networks, or data-broker SDKs that track user activity.

4. Children’s Privacy

Since our App collects no personal information whatsoever, it is fully safe for individuals of all ages, including children under 13.
